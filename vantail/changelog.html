What’s new in Vantail

New features, improvements and fixes, shipped every week.

Get updates by email

Releases

  1. v3.0.0 10 November 2026

    Preview environments for every branch

    Every branch now gets its own environment, not just pull requests. Push a branch and Vantail builds it on a stable URL that updates with each commit.

    • New Branch previews with stable URLs
    • New Private previews behind team sign-in
    • Improved Preview builds reuse the production cache
    • Fixed Preview links in reviews could point to an old build
  2. v2.8.0 21 October 2026

    Team dashboards

    A shared timeline of every deploy across your projects, with filters by author, branch and status.

    • New Team-wide deploy timeline
    • Improved Dashboard loads twice as fast on large teams
    • Fixed Times showed in UTC instead of your time zone
  3. v2.7.2 2 October 2026

    Faster rollbacks

    • Improved Rollbacks finish in under 4 seconds in every region
    • Improved vantail rollback shows the build it restores
    • Fixed Rare cache miss right after a rollback
  4. v2.7.0 15 September 2026

    Scheduled jobs

    Run tasks on a schedule next to your app. Jobs share your environment variables and stream their logs like any deploy.

    vantail.toml
    [[job]]
name     = "nightly-report"
schedule = "0 3 * * *"
command  = "node scripts/report.js"
    • New Scheduled jobs with retries
    • Fixed Log search ignored quoted phrases
  5. v2.6.0 28 August 2026

    Region pinning

    • New Pin server functions to one region for data rules
    • Improved Three new edge regions
