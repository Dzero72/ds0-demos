// about

We build the deploy tool we always wanted

Vantail is a small, remote team of engineers and designers who think shipping should be the calmest part of your week.

// our story

Born from one too many Friday freezes

In 2021, our founders spent a weekend rolling back a release by hand. The fix took ten minutes; finding the right build took six hours.

Vantail started as a script to make that never happen again. Today it ships millions of deploys a month for teams of every size, and it is still built around the same idea: every deploy should be easy to see and easy to undo.

// values

What we care about

Four ideas that shape every feature, review and support reply.

Calm by default

Good tools are quiet. We remove steps, alerts and settings until only the useful ones are left.

Everything undoable

People move faster when mistakes are cheap, so every action in Vantail can be reversed.

Write it down

We work in public docs and short written proposals, so anyone can join any decision.

Support is product

Engineers answer support every week. The fastest fix is the one that removes the question.

// timeline

How we got here

  1. March 2021

    The first script

    A 200-line deploy script, shared with a few friends, gets its first outside users.

  2. January 2022

    Vantail 1.0

    The CLI and hosted builds launch publicly. 1,000 teams sign up in the first month.

  3. June 2023

    Edge network

    Sites move to the edge, served from 20 regions with automatic certificates.

  4. April 2025

    Teams and dashboards

    Roles, single sign-on and the deploy dashboard bring the whole team into the loop.

  5. November 2026

    Vantail 3.0

    Previews for every branch, and 34 edge regions around the world.
