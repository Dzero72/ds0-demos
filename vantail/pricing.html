Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Contact us.

Is the Hobby plan really free?

Yes. Hobby is free for personal, non-commercial projects, with no time limit and no card needed.

How does per-seat billing work?

You pay for each person who can deploy. Viewers who only open previews and dashboards are free on every plan.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Any time. Moving to yearly applies the 20% discount straight away, and any unused monthly time is credited.

What happens if I go over my bandwidth?

Your site keeps running. We email you at 80% and 100%, and extra usage is billed at a flat rate per 100 GB on paid plans.

Do you offer discounts for open source and education?

Yes. Open-source projects and schools get Pro for free. Send us a link to your project through the contact page.

Need something bigger?

Custom contracts, invoicing and dedicated support for large teams.
